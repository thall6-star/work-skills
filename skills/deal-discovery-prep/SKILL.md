name: deal-discovery-prep
description: Invoke before a discovery call when a seller requests Discovery Prep, district research, current-state research, or "What we understand" slide bullets. Creates the Discovery Brief Word document.
---

## Purpose
Research a district before discovery and create the Discovery Brief: a Word document the rep uses on the call and the SE reads first. Research only. Do not mention {{PRODUCT}}, products, or solutions anywhere except the call sheet opening.

## Intake
- If the message does not include both a district and a state, reply once with only a bulleted list of these eight items, in this order, one item per bullet, exactly as written: "Discovery Prep." | "District:" | "State:" | "Discovery Date:" | "Attendees (name, title):" | "{{VENDOR}} Products:" | "What we know so far:" | "Links or attachments (budget book, board presentation, recent news):". No heading and no other words. Then stop. Do not research and do not create a document.
- If both a district and a state are present, start. Do not ask other intake questions. Leave missing optional fields blank in the document.
- If the district name matches more than one district in that state, ask which one.
- When a budget book or board presentation is attached, treat it as the primary source and use web research to add to it.

## Research Rules
1. Always run fresh public web research, even when a budget book is attached.
2. Source priority: attachments, then the district's budget book and budget pages, then other district pages and board materials found by search, then state education agency data, then local news. Do not take facts from vendor, lead-generation, or school-ranking sites.
3. Time period: the current and prior fiscal year for budget figures, up to five years for trends, and the last 12 months for news.
4. Use the latest-stage budget for the current fiscal year: adopted or approved before requested, requested before proposed. Label the stage wherever a budget figure appears.
5. Every number carries its fund, fiscal year, and source. Compare numbers only when fund, fiscal year, and definition match. When sources define a number differently, use the district's own figure and note the others in Issues to Explore.
6. Also look for: how the budget is organized (school allocations, student-based, program or department budgets, position-based staffing); enrollment and staffing trends; the fiscal year start and the district's budget calendar or dated budget milestones; each attendee's professional background; the district's current strategic direction.
7. Use `K-12 Finance Lens v1` to interpret findings. Do not take facts from it.

## Slide Bullet Rules
- What We Understand has exactly 3 bullets, each 10 to 25 words, written to the district ("you", "your").
- Bullet 1: a trend in the district's own numbers (enrollment, staffing, or revenue) with a percentage and the years.
- Bullet 2: the current budget situation, with the fund, fiscal year, and amount.
- Bullet 3: how the district budgets or allocates, or a budget decision under way.
- Facts only. No judgment words (struggling, challenge, inefficient, broken), no advice, no products.
- Every bullet traces to a line in Bullet Support.

## Attendee Research Rules
- Use a source about a person only if it names that person together with this district, such as a district bio or staff page, a budget book leadership page, board agenda or minutes, a district announcement, or a news article. A source that matches the name but not the district does not count.
- Do not use LinkedIn or other social media.
- Professional background only: current role, start year, prior roles and organizations. Nothing personal.
- If the title the rep gave differs from a public source, show both, for example "CFO (rep); Director, Budget and Management Services (district site)".
- If nothing qualifies, write "No public background found." Never fill a gap with a guess.

## Document
- Create a Word document named `<District>_<ST>_1_Discovery Brief.docx`. 3 pages maximum.

### Page 1: Rep Summary
- Page 1 should fit on one page. Keep each section within its own limit. If page 1 still runs long, shorten in this order: In the News, Strategic Direction, Who We're Meeting backgrounds. Never shorten Brief, What We Understand, or Budget Timing.
- Title: `<District full name> Discovery Brief`.
- **Opportunity** table, two columns (label, value): District (full name, city, state, NCES ID if found); Discovery Date; {{VENDOR}} Products.
- **District Facts** table, one row: Total Enrollment (with year and definition) | Schools (number of schools, not staff) | Total Budget (fund, FY, stage) | ERP (if publicly confirmed, otherwise "Unknown, ask Q2").
- **Brief**: 2 to 3 sentences on the central finance situation and what discovery should learn.
- **What We Know**: up to 3 bullets condensed from "What we know so far". Leave out if nothing was given.
- **What We Understand**: 3 bullets per the Slide Bullet Rules. Never shortened to fit the page.
- **Listen For**: 2 to 3 lines. Each names one call sheet question by number and says in one sentence why it matters for this district.
- **Budget Timing**: up to four labeled lines.
  - Fiscal Year: start and end month, with the source. If not found, write "Not found. Ask Q9."
  - Where They Are: one sentence placing the Discovery Date (or today's date if none was given) in the district's fiscal year by month number (the first month of the fiscal year is month 1), with any dated budget milestones found, labeled with their year.
  - Timing Read: one sentence beginning "Likely:". If a published district calendar shows next year's budget work under way, use Next-year request. Otherwise use the month number: months 1 to 3, Current-year funds; months 4 to 6, Either; months 7 to 12, Next-year request. If the fiscal year was not found, leave Timing Read out.
    - Current-year funds: funding would likely come from funds already identified or mid-year savings, with time to implement before next year's budget work.
    - Either: funding could come from current-year funds or mid-year savings, or from a line-item request for next year; Q9 decides.
    - Next-year request: funding would likely be a line-item request for next year's budget, and using the tool this cycle may require a parallel process.
  - Process Clues: up to 2 short bullets on what the calendar or budget book shows about how the budget is built, such as when principals or departments submit requests, when schools receive enrollment projections or staffing allocations, or how program budgets are packaged. Leave out if the sources show only generic dates.
- **Who We're Meeting** table: Name | Title | Background | Budget Lens. One row per attendee, up to 4. If the district's top finance official is not among the attendees, add one row for them marked "(not attending)". If no attendees were given, include only that row.
  - Background: 25 words or fewer, following the Attendee Research Rules.
  - Budget Lens: one sentence beginning "Likely:" on what this person most likely needs from budget development, staffing allocations, or budget visibility. Prefer a district-specific reason.
- **Strategic Direction**: one line naming the most recent strategic plan, board goals, or district improvement plan found, its type, its years, and its status: Current, Expired, In development, or Undated, with the source. Then its goal areas in the district's own words, up to 5, in one line. Leave out if none is found.
- **In the News**: up to 2 lines about budget, staffing, enrollment, campuses, or leadership from the last 12 months, each with date and publisher. Report what happened without characterizing it. Leave out if none is found.

### Page 2: Call Sheet
- Start on a new page. Print the opening and Q1 to Q9 exactly as written below, each followed by an empty "Notes:" line. Add no other questions.
Opening: My goal for this conversation is to learn about your district's financial needs and priorities to better understand if {{PRODUCT}} could be a fitting solution to complement your ERP. What are your goals for today's conversation?
Q1. What are some things you're looking to improve in your current budget and financial management process that we can talk about today?
Q2. What is your ERP system? Are you hosted or on prem (hosted on-site)?
Q3. Can you tell me about your current budget development process? What's your approach to budgeting? Do you do a rollover, use zero-based, or student based? ({{MODULE_1}})
Q4. What level of participation do you expect from departments during the budgeting process? Do departments help plan for staff, or just non-personnel? ({{MODULE_2}})
Q5. To what extent are schools and principals involved in budget development? Are you meeting with them regularly, do they have direct access to the tools used? Do they simply email requests? Lots of spreadsheet reconciliation? ({{MODULE_1}} and {{MODULE_2}})
Q6. If a budget manager needs to check an account balance, what does that process look like? Do they call you? Can they log into their ERP and view it directly? How frequently do staff look at their budgets and spend funds? ({{MODULE_2}})
Q7. Can you describe how you allocate dollars and staff to each school? ({{MODULE_3}})
Q8. Who is involved in the allocation process, and what systems or tools do you use to complete your allocations? ({{MODULE_3}})
Q9. Where are you in planning next year's budget, and how would something like this typically get funded? (Timing)

### Page 3: SE Layer
- Start on a new page. About half a page.
- **Public Data Depth**: one line: Rich, Moderate, or Thin, then what was found and what was not. Rich: budget figures plus a published budget process or calendar. Moderate: budget figures without a published process. Thin: little or no budget detail.
- **Alternate Bullets**: up to 2 swap-in bullets that follow the Slide Bullet Rules.
- **Bullet Support**: one line per slide bullet: the evidence, with any percentage first, then the source.
- **Issues to Explore**: up to 3. Issue in bold, then one line each for Evidence, What it could mean, and Question to validate.
- **Unknowns**: up to 3.
- **Clues to Confirm**: up to 3 product, vendor, or purchasing names seen on district sites (for example a staff portal or a purchasing cooperative), each labeled as a clue, not a fact. Leave out if none.
- **Sources**: up to 8, each as title, publisher, date (or "undated"), link. Only sources actually retrieved, including the source for each attendee background, the strategic direction, and the fiscal year.

## Final Check
- The district is correct.
- Every budget figure is from the latest-stage budget found and its stage is labeled.
- Exactly 3 slide bullets that follow the Slide Bullet Rules at full length.
- Every attendee background comes from a source that names that person with this district, or says "No public background found."
- Every Budget Lens and Timing Read begins with "Likely:", and the Timing Read follows the month rule.
- The call sheet shows the opening and Q1 to Q9 exactly as written.
- Optional sections with no evidence are left out, not filled.
- Every slide bullet passes the CFO Reread.
- {{PRODUCT}} and products appear only in the call sheet opening.
